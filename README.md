Room2Room is a UCLA-verified student marketplace designed to help students buy, sell, and exchange dorm supplies, furniture, textbooks, electronics, and other campus essentials. The platform promotes affordability, convenience, and sustainability by extending the life of items that would otherwise be discarded at the end of each academic year.

Overview

College students frequently face challenges when moving into or out of dorms and apartments. Many usable items are discarded because students lack a trusted, campus-specific marketplace to connect with one another. Room2Room addresses this problem by creating a secure platform exclusively for UCLA students, allowing them to exchange goods within their university community.

The platform emphasizes:

UCLA-only account verification
Secure user authentication
Item listings with images and descriptions
Student-to-student messaging
User reviews and ratings
Sustainable reuse of dorm and apartment supplies

Motivation

Room2Room was created to make student commerce safer and more accessible while reducing unnecessary waste generated during move-in and move-out periods. By connecting verified UCLA students, the platform creates a trusted environment for exchanging items that still have value.

Project Background

This repository is an independently maintained continuation of a project that was originally developed as a collaborative academic team project. The current repository was created to document, maintain, and further improve the platform beyond its original scope. While the initial concept and foundation were developed collaboratively, ongoing development, refinements, and future enhancements in this repository reflect my independent work and contributions.
